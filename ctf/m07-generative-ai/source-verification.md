# Source Verification Worksheet

## Checks

1. Does the claim originate from an official channel?
2. Can the sender be authenticated out of band?
3. Do metadata, timestamps and templates match known-good samples?
4. Are there signs of synthetic media generation?
5. Has a trusted role confirmed operational intent?

## Recommended LLM Use

Use the LLM to summarize contradictions and propose verification questions.
Do not use the LLM as the source of truth for authenticity.
