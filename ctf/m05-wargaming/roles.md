# Wargaming Roles

## Comandante Task Force
Maintains mission intent, prioritizes risk, authorizes operational changes.

## SOC
Confirms cyber evidence, recommends containment, preserves logs.

## Intelligence
Correlates OSINT, HUMINT, satellite and adversary intent.

## Public Affairs
Counters false narratives and protects credibility.

## Operations
Keeps patrol, rescue and navigation functions available.

## Legal
Validates ROE, release authority and evidence handling.
